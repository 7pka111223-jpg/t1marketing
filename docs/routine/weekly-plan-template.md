# Weekly plan template

The shape of every `Week of …` tab in T1 Weekly Plans. Section order and table columns are fixed; the worked example for the week of 5 Oct 2026 is `docs/prototypes/PROTOTYPE-weekly-plan.md`.

## Header (before section 1)

- One line: `Written by the Friday routine on <Friday date>. Nothing here is published until it is approved.`
- **Skills this week:** the skill each post covers, one per post, each different.
- **Approve by:** Saturday night. The team films Saturday and Sunday; the first post is Monday 7:30 PM.

## 1. Last week at a glance

Covers the Plan Week that ended last Sunday.

| Post | Pillar | Reach | Avg watch | Skip rate | Saves | Shares | Leads (keyword) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

Then two lines:
- **Leads:** new Leads by Session Type (Group / Private / Undecided), Free Assessments booked, Outcomes (Joined Group / Joined Private).
- **What it means:** one or two sentences naming what to repeat and what to change this week.

## 2. This week's plan

| Day | Cairo time | Format | Pillar | Concept | Lead Keyword | Offer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Posting slots (strategy §27): Monday 7:30 PM, Wednesday 10:00 AM, Friday 5:30 PM, Sunday 7:30 PM. Three Reels and one Carousel, plus a Stories row.

## 3. Filming list

| When | Branch | Who | Shots | For |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Weekend slots first (Saturday and Sunday sessions), since the first post is Monday. End with the line: `Don't film the Ladies-Only Session (6–7 PM) unless everyone in frame is on the roster with consent Yes.`

## 4. Developed pieces

- **Reels:** the strategy §39 fields: concept name, objective, pillar, audience, format and length, why it should work, hook and first frame, a beat table (time | beat), cover, caption (Egyptian Arabic + English, engagement CTA then one offer line), Story follow-up, primary KPI, one-line TikTok note.
- **Carousel:** a slide table (slide | content), caption, KPI.
- **Swap-in:** every post that depends on a moment (first rep, PR) names its Swap-in: concept, pillar, keyword, offer, and footage that is certain.
- **Reuse:** a Sequel or Story Reshare cites its source post's permalink.

## 5. Stories bank

| Slot | Options |
|---|---|

Slots: 12:00 live gym · 15:00 interactive · 18:30 proof · 21:00 answer · 22:00 conversion (Sat, Tue, Thu only). The Story Reshare, if any, goes here with its permalink.

## 6. Checks the routine ran

A checklist, each item ticked only after it was verified against the plan:
- No price in any caption, Story or on-screen text.
- Every named person is on the roster with consent Yes.
- No two posts share a Lead Keyword; each keyword matches its post's skill.
- The humor post carries no offer.
- Every moment-dependent post has a Swap-in.
- At most one Sequel and one Story Reshare; no source post reused within 12 weeks.
- Nothing scheduled or published.

End the tab with one line: `Reuse log: <permalink(s) reused this week, or "none">`.
