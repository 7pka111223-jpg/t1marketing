# Weekly plan template

The shape of every `Week of …` tab in T1 Weekly Plans. Section order and table columns are fixed; the content is new every week.

## Header (before section 1)

- One line: `Written by the Friday routine on <Friday date>. Nothing here is published until it is approved.`
- **Plan Week N**, and `Launch Week N of 4` during Launch Weeks.
- **Skills this week:** the skill each Gym post covers, one per post, each different.
- **Approve by:** Saturday night. The team films Saturday and Sunday; the first post is Monday 8:00 PM.
- **Setup still open** (only while T1 Context section 7 says so): Online Coaching Highlights, the three pinned posts.

## 1. Last week at a glance

Covers the Plan Week that ended last Sunday.

| Post | Lane | Pillar | Hook type | Reach | Avg watch | Skip rate | Saves | Shares | Leads (keyword) |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

Hook type is for Online posts; write "–" for Gym posts.

Then three lines:
- **Leads:** new Leads by Session Type (Group / Private / Online / Undecided), Free Assessments booked, Outcomes (Joined Group / Joined Private / Joined Online), website clicks for the week.
- **What it means, Gym:** one or two sentences naming what to repeat and what to change this week.
- **What it means, Online:** the same for the Online Lane.

## 4. Developed pieces

- **Gym Reels:** the §39 fields: concept name, objective, pillar, audience, format and length, why it should work, hook and first frame, a beat table (time | beat), cover, caption (Egyptian Arabic + English, engagement CTA then one offer line), Story follow-up, primary KPI, one-line TikTok note.
- **Online Reels:** the online §39 fields: concept name, objective, pillar, hook type, audience, format and length, why it should work, hook, a beat table (time | beat), coach role, athlete role, app screen requirements, on-screen text, editing notes, cover (online §34 family), caption (online §36: problem, insight, why it matters, CTA), offer line `DM <KEYWORD>`, Story follow-up, primary KPI (online §43), one-line TikTok note.
- **Carousels:** a slide table (slide | content), caption, KPI.
- **Swap-in:** every post that depends on a moment (first rep, PR) or a missing input (Proof Record, demo account) names its Swap-in: concept, pillar, keyword, offer, and footage that is certain.
- **Reuse:** a Sequel or Story Reshare cites its source post's permalink.

## 6. Checks the routine ran

A checklist, each item ticked only after it was verified against the plan:
- No price in any caption, Story or on-screen text.
- Every named person is on the roster with consent Yes.
- No two posts share a Lead Keyword; each Gym keyword matches its post's skill, each Online keyword is COACHING, ONLINE or PLAN.
- The humor post carries no offer.
- Every Online claim is on T1 Context section 7's available list; none is on its not-available list.
- Every result shown comes from a Proof Record.
- Every moment-dependent or input-dependent post has a Swap-in.
- At most one Sequel and one Story Reshare; no source post reused within 12 weeks.
- Nothing scheduled or published.

## Strategy check (every 4th Plan Week only)

Gym Lane: median reach, average watch time and 3-second skip rate of the last four Plan Weeks' Gym Reels against the §6 baseline, plus Leads per 1,000 reach by pillar. Online Lane: Online Leads per 1,000 reach by pillar and by hook family, plus website clicks. One line on what each strategy file's time-sensitive sections (§47, online §49) should update. The routine suggests; the owner edits the strategy files.

End the tab with one line: `Reuse log: <permalink(s) reused this week, or "none">`.
