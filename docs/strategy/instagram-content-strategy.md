# 2. Core Positioning

TripleOneBars should not behave like a generic gym social account.

The Instagram should feel like:

> **The calisthenics crew you want to be part of.**

Underneath that:

> **The place that can actually teach you the skills.**

The account should combine:

- real athletes
- recognizable coaches
- skill mastery
- progression
- humor
- inside-gym culture
- challenges
- real reactions
- community
- Egyptian identity
- high-level calisthenics
- beginner accessibility

The page should feel **athlete-first**, not advertisement-first.

---

# 3. Main Creative Principle

The strongest current TripleOneBars content pattern is:

> **SKILL + PEOPLE + PARTICIPATION**

Strong content should usually contain at least two of these:

- visually interesting calisthenics skill
- recognizable athlete
- recognizable coach
- challenge
- competition
- progression
- comparison
- inside joke
- real reaction
- viewer question
- invitation to tag someone
- level selection
- community participation

Generic motivation should not be the default.

---

# 4. Brand Personality on Instagram

TripleOneBars should feel:

- bold
- energetic
- athletic
- disciplined
- inclusive
- direct
- confident
- human
- culturally natural
- competitive without being hostile
- serious about training but not overly serious socially

The desired viewer reaction is:

> **"I want to train with these people."**

Not just:

> **"That gym looks professional."**

# 10. Pillar — T1 Skills / Challenges

## Purpose

Generate high-reach content around controlled, visually interesting calisthenics skills.

## Formats

- CAN YOU DO THIS?
- ONE BAR. 3 LEVELS.
- COACH VS ATHLETE
- ATHLETE VS ATHLETE
- WHO HAS THE CLEANEST REP?
- 3 WAYS TO MUSCLE-UP
- GUESS THE SKILL
- SKILL LADDER
- WARRIOR → SPARTAN → LEGEND
- T1 TEST
- BEAT THE COACH
- TEAM CHALLENGE

## Topics

- pull-up variations
- muscle-up variations
- handstand levels
- L-sit progressions
- front lever progressions
- controlled dips
- core holds
- grip challenges
- dead hang challenge
- strict rep challenge
- tempo challenge
- no-swing challenge

## Rules

- show the skill immediately
- avoid long intros
- let movement carry the Reel
- show reactions
- create a viewer question
- make levels clear
- emphasize control and form
- do not glorify reckless stunts

# 11. Pillar — T1 Characters / Humor

- "Easy."

- who cheats reps?
- who is always late?
- who talks most?
- who takes the longest rest?

Use naturally recurring personalities.

Do not force a personality that the audience does not recognize.

### COACH VS ATHLETE

Same challenge. Same skill.

### POV

Examples:

- POV: coach says "last set"
- POV: someone mentions conditioning
- POV: coach turns around
- POV: you finally hit the PR
- POV: beginner says "I can't do pull-ups"

# 12. Pillar — Learn the Skill

## Purpose

Build coaching authority and saveable content.

TripleOneBars should prove it can teach, not only entertain.

## Series

### YOUR FIRST ___

- pull-up
- dip
- handstand
- muscle-up
- L-sit

### FIX THIS

- fix your pull-up
- fix your dip
- fix your handstand line
- fix your muscle-up transition
- fix your core tension

### T1 BREAKDOWN

One technical concept explained by a coach.

### ONE CUE

Very short coaching advice.

Example:

> Can't get your chest to the bar?  
> Drive your elbows down.

### 3 MISTAKES

Make each mistake visual.

Do not turn the Reel into a lecture.

# 19. Default Reel Structure

## 0–2 sec

Hook.

Strong visual immediately.

## 2–6 sec

Problem, attempt, challenge, setup, or joke.

## 6–18 sec

Explanation, demonstration, competition, or escalation.

## 18–25 sec

Payoff.

Correct rep, winner, transformation, or reaction.

## 25–30 sec

CTA where needed.

Not every Reel should reach 30 seconds.

Humor may work best at 8–18 seconds.

Skill content can run longer if retention supports it.

# 21. Language System

TripleOneBars should use natural Egyptian Arabic + English.

Do not translate English marketing copy literally.

Write from the same creative idea.

Example:

> مشكلتك مش إنك ضعيف.  
> أنت غالباً بتعمل الـ transition غلط.  
> جرّب الـ drill ده قبل الـ muscle-up attempts.  
> 3 sets. Clean reps.  
> وبعدها قولي الفرق.

Technical terms can stay English when that matches real gym language.

Common terms:

- pull-up
- muscle-up
- handstand
- transition
- set
- reps
- skill
- coach
- warm-up
- core
- tension
- level
- clean reps
- next session
- drill

Avoid forced formal Arabic terminology that athletes do not naturally use.

# 32. Creative Do / Don't

## Do

- lead with movement
- use real members
- use real coaches
- show reactions
- use Egyptian humor
- create recurring formats
- make personalities recognizable
- show real progression
- invite participation
- keep captions concise
- use collaboration opportunities
- show high-level skills alongside beginner accessibility

## Don't

- write generic motivational essays
- overuse "no excuses"
- repeat "join the family" on every post
- make every Reel cinematic
- hide personality behind graphics
- use long intros
- explain before showing
- make every post an advertisement
- copy trends without adapting them to T1 culture
