# TripleOne Marketing OS

Marketing for TripleOneBars, a calisthenics gym in Egypt: planning, producing and measuring Instagram and TikTok content whose business purpose is bringing new people into paid training.

## Leads

**Lead**:
A person who DMs a Lead Keyword, books a Free Assessment, or signs up through the Online Coaching page on tripleonebars.com. One Lead per person per Session Type, credited to their first Lead Keyword; an Existing Member counts only for a Session Type they don't already have.
_Avoid_: Prospect, inquiry, conversion (a Conversion is the later paid outcome)

**Lead Keyword**:
The topic word a post asks people to DM (e.g. "PULL-UP", "MU", "START"). Always shown in English and logged in its canonical spelling, it identifies the post that produced the Lead. Gym posts use skill keywords; Online Coaching posts use only COACHING, ONLINE or PLAN.
_Avoid_: DM code, trigger word

**Comment Keyword**:
A word a post asks people to comment for engagement (e.g. "Comment MU"). Commenting it never makes someone a Lead.

**Free Assessment**:
The free first session booked on tripleonebars.com; the single entry point for Group and Private Sessions, confirmed afterwards by the team. Online Coaching has no Free Assessment.
_Avoid_: Trial, consult, intro session

**Existing Member**:
A person already training at TripleOneBars in some Session Type. Flagged on any Lead they become.

**Session Type**:
Which paid offer a Lead is interested in: a Group Session, a Private Session or Online Coaching, or Undecided until they answer.

**Group Session**:
A coached class of several athletes on a shared schedule.
_Avoid_: Class, group training

**Ladies-Only Session**:
A Group Session reserved for women, on its own schedule.
_Avoid_: Women's class, girls' session

**Private Session**:
One-to-one coaching with a named coach, sold as a multi-session package.
_Avoid_: PT, personal training, 1:1

**Online Coaching**:
Remote coaching through the TripleOne coaching webapp: a coach assigns dated workouts (exercises, sets, reps, rest) and the athlete completes them and leaves notes. Logged as Session Type "Online".
_Avoid_: App subscription, online program, workout PDF

**Proof Record**:
A real, measured before-and-after result (e.g. 3 → 8 pull-ups in 6 weeks) for a named person with on-camera consent, listed in T1 Context. The only source of numbers a Proof post may show.
_Avoid_: Testimonial, transformation

## Planning

**Lane**:
Which program a feed post sells: **Gym** (Group and Private Sessions) or **Online** (Online Coaching). Both Lanes share one account and one weekly plan.
_Avoid_: Channel, stream

**Launch Weeks**:
The first four Plan Weeks that carry the Online Lane: three Online posts and two Gym posts a week, following the Online Coaching strategy's launch campaign. Afterwards the Online Lane drops to two posts and the Gym Lane goes back to three.

**Plan Week**:
The Monday-to-Sunday span one weekly plan covers, delivered the Friday before so the weekend is free for filming.
_Avoid_: Content week, Sat-Fri week

**Swap-in**:
A named replacement post, built from footage that is certain, for any planned post that depends on a moment that may not happen (a first rep, a PR).
_Avoid_: Backup post, fallback

**Sequel**:
A new post in a series that already performed on the account, made with fresh footage (e.g. a second round of coach introductions, or a "then/now" pairing of an old clip with a new attempt).
_Avoid_: Remake, rerun

**Story Reshare**:
A past feed post shared again to Stories, at least 8 weeks after it was published.
_Avoid_: Repost, throwback post
