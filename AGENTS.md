# TripleOne Marketing OS agent notes

- Preserve human approval gates for public publishing, DMs, email sends, and spend.
- Existing TripleOne media is the first choice for creative assets.
- Brand voice: direct, confident, athletic, respectful; short active sentences.
- Main objectives: brand awareness and memberships.
- Primary channels: Instagram and TikTok.
- Languages: natural Egyptian Arabic + English; never literal translation by default.
- Keep incremental operating cost at or under $20/month unless explicitly approved.
- Reuse the existing Supabase project; keep marketing records in the `marketing` schema.
- Do not expose sensitive member data to AI workflows. Prefer aggregated/read-only attribution views.
